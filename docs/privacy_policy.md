Privacy Policy — SimplyJobTracker
Last updated: September 24, 2026

SimplyJobTracker is designed to keep your data private and on your device.

What we collect: SimplyJobTracker does not collect, transmit, or store any of your job application data (company names, notes, interview details, ratings) outside your device. All app data is stored locally using Apple's SwiftData framework and is never sent to us or any third party.

In-app purchases: SimplyJobTracker offers an optional tip jar. Purchases are processed through Apple's App Store and RevenueCat, a third-party subscription management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous user ID and purchase history to sync and validate your purchase — no financial information (card numbers, billing details) is ever seen by us. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy for details on their data handling.

No tracking, no ads, no analytics: SimplyJobTracker contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics/tracking SDKs, and does not share data with data brokers or advertising networks.

Your control: You can export your data as a CSV file or permanently delete all app data at any time from the Settings tab.
