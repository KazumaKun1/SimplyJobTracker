## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is my data backed up anywhere?**
No — SimplyJobTracker stores everything locally on your device. If you delete the app or lose your device without a backup, your data cannot be recovered. Use **Settings → Export as CSV** to keep a copy of your applications.

**Does the app require an account?**
No. SimplyJobTracker works entirely offline with no sign-in required.

**How do I remove all my data?**
Go to **Settings → Clear all data**. This permanently deletes every application on your device and cannot be undone.

**What does the "AI Health Check" feature do, and why don't I see it?**
It's an on-device summary of an application's status and suggested next step, powered by Apple's on-device AI (Foundation Models). It only appears on devices running iOS 26 or later with Apple Intelligence enabled — it will not appear on unsupported devices.

**What is the tip jar in Settings?**
An optional way to support development of the app. It has no effect on any app functionality — everything works the same whether or not you tip.

**Does the widget update automatically?**
Yes, the home screen widget refreshes whenever you add, edit, or delete an application in the app.
